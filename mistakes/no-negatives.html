3.1A rubric that can only reward, never penalise

Costs pointsRubric Criteria - Overall Rubric Quality

The mistake

Too few negatives for the scenario's traps, so taking the bait costs nothing extra, or a negative worded as the virtue, which marks down correct runs.

Do this instead

After Leg A, write a negative for each worthwhile wrong turn Model A took or the setup genuinely invites, stating the flaw, never the virtue.

Where it shows

Subjective criterion 10-3
In plate12_simplification_lesson.mp4, the finished teaching plate remains legible at normal 1080p or full-window playback…

What it should have been

Subjective criterion 10-3
In plate12_simplification_lesson.mp4, the finished teaching plate renders with its captions or labels below legible size at normal 1080p or full-window playback.

How it breaks the standardA negative takes its weight when Present, so subjective criterion 10 charges every legible video three points and spares an unreadable one. The golden, the reference the subjective block is graded against, is the video it penalises. Subjective criterion 3 has the right form: it names the flaw, a black or frozen frame.

The Green Shell rule it breaksGuidelines · 5.5 Rating Model A and Justifying the Failures The run contains the undesirable behavior or boundary violation described by the criterion Present on a negative is the failure, so it names the flaw.

Catch it in your own task

Negatives should be about a quarter of the set. Present means failure, so a negative describing a good run is inverted.

Where the Green Shell guidance is written

Section titles as they print in the Green Shell Guidelines and QC spec, so you can search for them.

Guidelines · 5.3 Positive and Negative Distribution

Negatives are the exception: They should usually make up around 25% of the rubric block and should generally stay below 30%. Use a negative criterion only when it captures a specific, plausible failure mode that the scenario or trajectory meaningfully exposes.

Guidelines · 5.2 Rubric Weights

The negative slots are for attractive wrong paths the model has to resist.

Guidelines · 5.5 Rating Model A and Justifying the Failures

For negative weight: The run contains the undesirable behavior or boundary violation described by the criterion

Present on a negative is the failure, so a negative names the flaw.

Guidelines · 5.7 Final Rubric Checklist

Negatives are about a quarter of the block, and each names a failure the setup genuinely invites.
Why it matters

A scenario earns its difficulty from the wrong answers sitting next to the right ones: the superseded quote, the decoy invoice, the figure from a different part of the store. Without negatives the rubric cannot see any of them, so a run that walks into every trap scores the same as one that never went near a trap. They are also the cheapest criteria to write, because the Model A trajectory usually shows you which failure to write about.
