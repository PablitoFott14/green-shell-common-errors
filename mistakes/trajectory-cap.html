3.4More trajectory criteria than the Guidelines allow

Fails the taskRubric Criteria - 80/20 Outcome Split (Process Over Cap)

The mistake

Process criteria past 20% of the objective block. Every Trajectory target counts, with Tool Use, Agent Behavior and narration of the process. Each is usually sound on its own, so the count creeps up.

Do this instead

Keep only what no deliverable can show, such as a load bearing media read, and move the rest's values into completion criteria on the artifact.

The Green Shell rule it breaksGuidelines · 5.7 Final Rubric Checklist Every process criterion has been tried as a completion criterion first, and only kept if no deliverable can show the failure. And ten Trajectory criteria stay inside the 20% cap only in a block of 50 or more objective criteria.

Catch it in your own task

Count the objective criteria, take 20% and round down: 12 allow 2, 42 allow 8. Then count every Trajectory, Tool Use and Agent Behavior row.

Where the Green Shell guidance is written

Section titles as they print in the Green Shell Guidelines and QC spec, so you can search for them.

Guidelines · 5.1 Rubric Fundamentals

MANDATORY: at least 80% of the objective criteria grade completion. At most 20% grade process. If the process share is above 20%, the task fails automatically.

The 80/20 rule. Zero process criteria is the preferred outcome.

Guidelines · 5.1 Rubric Fundamentals

Round down: with 12 objective criteria, at most 2 can be process (20% of 12 is 2.4).

Guidelines · 5.4 Category & Evaluation Target

An identification, a dependency order, a piece of restraint. The intent it pursued, never a named tool call. Every Trajectory criterion counts toward the 20% process cap (section 5.1).

What belongs on the Trajectory target.

QC spec · Rubric Criteria - 80/20 Outcome Split (Process Over Cap)

More than 20% of the objective criteria (by count, rounded down) grade process rather than outcomes.

The failing option, Fail - Process Over Cap.

Guidelines · 5.7 Final Rubric Checklist

Every process criterion has been tried as a completion criterion first, and only kept if no deliverable can show the failure.
Why it matters

The client grades completion: what was delivered, not the route. Past the cap the set grades the route instead of the result, and the same evidence usually appears twice: once as a trajectory criterion about the read, once as an artifact criterion about the value it produced. Red Shell capped trajectory criteria at five; in Green Shell, process criteria above 20% of the objective block fail the task automatically, and zero is preferred.
