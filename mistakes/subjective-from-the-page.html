5.3Subjective criteria that require your golden's wording

Costs pointsRubric Criteria - Subjective Block Scope

The mistake

A subjective criterion requires the exact heading or label your golden happens to use, words the prompt does not contain.

Do this instead

Name the element by what it is, and grade how it is presented, not what it is called.

Example 22Red ShellSmall Business & Personal Docs · 3 Leg A turns

What is wrongNine of ten subjective criteria require the golden page's own headings and labels, such as “Claim 1 - May trip”, which none of the three Leg A prompts asks for.

Leg A turns 1 to 3 ask for reimbursement_status.html: for each of two mileage claims, whether the money reached Noor, and the deadline for an overdue one.

Where it shows

Subjective criterion 1+3
In reimbursement_status.html, two claim cards sit in one row, headed Claim 1 - May trip on the left and Claim 2 - August trip on the right.
Subjective criterion 4+3
…a four-column table has the header row Claim, Reached your account?, Due by 28 Aug?, and Verdict.
Leg A prompts
None of the three asks for these headings.

What it should have been

Subjective criterion 1+3
In reimbursement_status.html, the two claims are presented as two cards side by side rather than one after the other.
Subjective criterion 4+3
In reimbursement_status.html, the determination table's header row is set apart from its data rows by colour or weight.

How it breaks the standardNo Leg A prompt asks for these headings; they are the golden's choices. Each criterion copies the golden page, so nothing flags it, but a correct page with its own headings fails subjective criteria 1 and 4 and seven more. Only subjective criterion 2 names a property without quoting text.

Where the Green Shell guidance is written

QC spec · Rubric Criteria - Subjective Block Scope

Every subjective criterion names one observable property of the rendered artifact, graded relative to the golden.

The passing option.

Guidelines · 7.1 Writing Subjective Rubrics

If anything related to the render is asked in the prompt, it requires an objective rubric ONLY.

Guidelines · 5.6 How the Client Defines Rubric Issue Severity

The criterion can be rated, but it is built wrong: redundant, vague, misplaced, misweighted or pointed at the wrong thing.

Moderate, which fails the task above 15%. Its named types include Overlapping or Redundant, Overfitting and Underfitting, Incorrect Weights, Block Bleed and Miscategorized.

Why it matters

Subjective rubrics grade how the artifact reads: layout, hierarchy, spacing, consistency. A criterion naming one build's text rejects a subset of valid implementations, and a second correct run presenting the same results under its own wording fails almost the whole block. Overfitting counts toward the share of criteria built wrong, and the task fails once that share passes 15% of all its criteria.

Leg A turn 1
It's the 28th of August and I'm trying to close out something that's been sitting in the back of my head. I drove my own car to Everett twice this year for work, once in May and once in August, and I filed a mileage claim for each trip. Marguerite approved both of them and HR wrote back saying they were acknowledged and complete and that the money comes through payroll, no separate deposit, nothing else needed from me. Which is fine, except my paycheck is the same number every single time and I honestly can't tell whether either one of those ever actually landed. I put some things in inputs for you, the expense policy and a couple of my pay statements, plus something the bank sent me. My mail and my accounts should have the rest. Can you work out, for each of the two claims separately, whether the money reached me or not? I don't want them added together into one number, I want to know which one is which. And if one of them didn't come through, tell me whether I can still do anything about it, because I have a feeling the policy puts a clock on that. Put it together as reimbursement_status.html. I want to be able to open it and read it properly before I send anything to Payroll.
Leg A turn 2
I found another pay statement and attached it as IMG_4471.jpg. Please check it against the policy, the claim emails, and the account records, then update the same reimbursement_status.html with whatever it changes. Keep the two claims separate and preserve everything that remains supported.
Leg A turn 3
Before I send this, for any claim you determine is overdue and unpaid, put the discrepancy deadline on the page as an exact date and say whether it had already passed as of the 28th. Keep using reimbursement_status.html, keep the two claims separate, and preserve everything that remains supported.
