Why “due” means 12 June and 4 September

Policy §4.2 says approved mileage appears on the second regular (biweekly) payroll date after your program manager approves it — not the first, and not a separate deposit (§4.1). Your payroll lands on alternating Fridays.

Important — being “acknowledged” does not protect you. HR’s note that both claims are “acknowledged and complete” only means a claim number was assigned (§3.3) and approval is on file. Policy §5.3 is explicit: after 90 days the claim is administratively closed and cannot be reopened, “no exception… for claims that were acknowledged as received or recorded as approved.” The responsibility to confirm payment and to report a miss in writing is yours (§5.1–5.2). So the May claim really can be lost if you let 10 September pass.
