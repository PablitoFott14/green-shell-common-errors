6 · How to scribe the edges that meet the wall & ceiling

Build the box dead square. At 1935 wide it drops in with ~20 mm each side at the floor; side and ceiling gaps close with three scribed parts.

6.1 · End gaps → door frame & corner (scribe fillers)

The opening is 30 wider at the ceiling, so each end has a wedge gap ~20 (floor) → 35 (ceiling). A 18 mm gable edge can't fill that — a scribe filler does.

  1. Each filler = 2250 × 50, glued to the gable's front outer edge, proud into the gap.
  2. Carcass plumb, ~20 gap each side at floor; clamp filler flush; scribe to the largest overhang (~35 at ceiling).
  3. Cut, back-bevel 3–5°, plane to the line, refit; fix into the gable — never the plaster.

6.2 · Top → ceiling (separate strip)

  1. Carcass top 2350; ceiling 2390→2400. The 1935 × 80 strip laps up, scribed to the ceiling (≥20 allowance).

6.3 · Bottom → skirting (notched plinth)

  1. Skirting 75 high, ~8 proud; plinth back rail notched 75 × 8 to tuck over it.
